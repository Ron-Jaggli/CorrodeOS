Capabilities
============

On Linux a program may try to do anything, and the kernel checks who it
is running as. Root may do everything; everyone else may do what the
permission bits allow.

Corrode has no users and no root. A program can do exactly what it holds a
capability for, and nothing else. A capability names one thing -- a window,
a pool of memory, the list of running tasks -- and says what the holder may
do with it: read it, write it, or hand it on.

caps            lists what this terminal holds, slot by slot.

grant COMMAND   asks for what COMMAND needs and then runs it. The kernel
                shows a prompt that no program can draw or answer: only a
                click on Allow, by you, hands the capability over. The
                terminal gets exactly that one thing, without the right to
                pass it on.

revoke [WHAT]   drops what grant gave, and the terminal is back to what it
                started with.

grant is to sudo what a key to one door is to a master key.
