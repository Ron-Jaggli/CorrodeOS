Keys
====

Left, Right      move along the line
Home, End        to the start or end of it (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+E too)
Up, Down         earlier lines, and back
Tab              finish a command's name
Ctrl+U, Ctrl+K   cut to the start, or to the end
Ctrl+W           cut the word before the cursor
Ctrl+C           abandon the line, or stop a sleep
Ctrl+L           clear the screen
